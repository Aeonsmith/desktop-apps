$$S_{\text{5D}} = -\frac{1}{16\pi \tilde{G}} \int d^5 x \sqrt{|-\tilde{g}|} \tilde{R}$$

$$S_{\text{4D}} = -\frac{1}{16\pi G} \int d^4 x \sqrt{|-g|} R - \frac{1}{4} \int d^4 x \sqrt{|-g|} F_{\mu\nu} F^{\mu\nu}$$
where $G = \tilde{G} / (2\pi R)$ is Newton's 4D gravitational constant.

### 3. Topological Momentum & Charge Quantization

$$p_\theta = \frac{n}{R}, \quad n \in \mathbb{Z}$$

$$m_n = \frac{|n|}{R}, \quad q_n = n e = \frac{n \kappa}{R}$$
